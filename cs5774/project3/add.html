How should I score?
  1. Fully correct: meets every part of the expected behavior.
  2. Partly correct: right idea, but a noticeable error changes the result.
  3. Mostly wrong: major errors or ignores key instructions.
  4. Unusable: off-topic, fabricated, or does not run.
